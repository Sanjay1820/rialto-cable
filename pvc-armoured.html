PVC Armoured Cable

PVC Armoured Cable from Rialto Cables is designed for efficient and reliable power distribution in a variety of applications. Manufactured with high-grade PVC (Polyvinyl Chloride) insulation and sheathing, these cables offer excellent protection against moisture, abrasion, and environmental factors. Lightweight and easy to install, Rialto’s PVC Armoured cables are ideal for indoor wiring, control panels, and light industrial use, ensuring consistent performance and long-lasting durability.

Product Range:

Low-voltage power cable with PVC insulation and steel armouring for mechanical protection. Suitable for underground, outdoor, and industrial installations. Offers reliable performance in power distribution where moderate thermal resistance is acceptable. Rated voltage: 1.1kV.
