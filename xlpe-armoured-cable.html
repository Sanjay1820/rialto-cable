XLPE Armoured Cable

XLPE Armoured Cable from Rialto Cables is designed for efficient and reliable power distribution in a variety of applications. Manufactured with high-grade PVC (Polyvinyl Chloride) insulation and sheathing, these cables offer excellent protection against moisture, abrasion, and environmental factors. Lightweight and easy to install, Rialto’s PVC unarmoured cables are ideal for indoor wiring, control panels, and light industrial use, ensuring consistent performance and long-lasting durability.

Product Range:

Durable low-voltage power cable designed for industrial and utility applications. Features XLPE insulation for high thermal resistance and steel armouring for mechanical protection. Suitable for direct burial, underground, or outdoor installations in harsh environments. Rated voltage: 1.1kV.
