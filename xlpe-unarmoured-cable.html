XLPE Unarmoured Cable

XLPE Unarmoured Cable from Rialto Cables is designed for efficient and reliable power distribution in a variety of applications. Manufactured with high-grade PVC (Polyvinyl Chloride) insulation and sheathing, these cables offer excellent protection against moisture, abrasion, and environmental factors. Lightweight and easy to install, Rialto’s PVC unarmoured cables are ideal for indoor wiring, control panels, and light industrial use, ensuring consistent performance and long-lasting durability.

Product Range:

Low-voltage power cable with XLPE insulation, offering excellent thermal and electrical properties. Ideal for indoor fixed installations in conduits or cable trays. Suitable for industrial and commercial applications where mechanical protection is not required. Rated voltage: 1.1kV.
