RV-K XLPE Cables

3 Core & 4 Core Round

RV-K cable is a flexible, low-voltage power cable designed for industrial power distribution systems. It is constructed with electrolytic annealed copper conductors and insulated with cross-linked polyethylene (XLPE) and an outer sheath of PVC or equivalent thermoplastic. It offers excellent mechanical strength, flexibility, and resistance to moisture, chemicals, and UV radiation.

Product Range:

Rialto RV-K Pump Cables are heavy-duty, flexible power cables designed for submersible motors and demanding installations. They offer excellent resistance to water, oil, and mechanical stress.

TECHNICAL DATA
Operating Temperature: -15°C to +90°C
Conductor: Electrolytic annealed flexible copper (Class 5)
Test Voltage: 0.6/1 kV
Min. Bending Radius: 5 × outer diameter
Flame Propagation: IEC 60332-1 compliant
